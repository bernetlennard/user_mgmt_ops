TARGETS showing <unknown> means metrics-server is not answering, not that the load is zero.
A scale-out that never happens despite high cpu is usually the ResourceQuota, which reports it
on the ReplicaSet rather than on the HPA:

  kubectl -n {{ .Release.Namespace }} describe rs -l app.kubernetes.io/component=backend | grep -i quota

spec.replicas is absent from the backend Deployment while the HPA is enabled, so that ArgoCD's
selfHeal does not revert scale-outs.

values-prod.yaml / values-staging.yaml.
